To give a screen a name in the list (e.g. "Kitchen tablet"), add ?name=Kitchen%20tablet to the end of the address the first time you open it. On that screen, Test this device (bottom-right corner) plays a short chime, so you know its sound works.

Phones and laptops only play the azan while the page is open, and some browsers need one tap on the page before they're allowed to play sound. For a TV, the Smart Azan TV app is best: it wakes the TV and plays even when you're watching something else.
